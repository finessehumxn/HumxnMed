# HumxnMed — "How to use" voiceover scripts

Pronounce **HumxnMed = "Human-Med."** Tone: warm, calm, confident. `[brackets]` are optional
on-screen/pacing cues — don't read them aloud. Each is ~60–90 seconds at an easy pace.

---

## SCRIPT A — For clinicians (HumxnMed Clinical)

Welcome to HumxnMed Clinical — your companion at the point of care.

[PAUSE]

Here's how to use it in about a minute.

First — just open it. Go to humxnmed-dot-millennials-creatives-dot-com, slash, console… in any browser, on your phone or a computer. There's nothing to install.

If you're a founding clinician, activate your access. Go to slash-redeem, type in the founder code you were given, and you'll see "Clinical is activated." No account required — and it works on any device you enter it on.

[PAUSE]

Now, pick the tool you need. There are four, right across the top.

**Look up** — for rapid, sourced reference at the bedside. Drugs, protocols, workups — attributed, so you can verify it.

**Document** — turn your rough or dictated notes into a clean, structured draft you review and sign. It organizes what you said; it won't invent findings.

**Explain** — put the diagnosis or the plan into plain language for your patient and their family, in the moment, in any language.

And **Weigh** — considerations to weigh. A differential, workup options, and the basis behind each one — so it supports your judgment. You're always the decision-maker.

[PAUSE]

To use any of them, just type… or tap Dictate and speak. Take your time — pauses are fine, it won't cut you off. Tap the mic again when you're done, review your text, and hit the button. It never sends anything on its own.

Working hands-free? Turn on Read-aloud to hear the answer back.

And if you'd like your access to follow you across devices, sign in with your email and a six-digit code. You never need it to use the console — it just remembers you.

[PAUSE]

One thing to always keep in mind: HumxnMed supports your judgment. It gives you information and drafting you review. It is not a diagnosis, and it is not a medical device. You decide.

That's it. Open the console, and let's get to work.

## SCRIPT B — For patients & families

Welcome to HumxnMed — your health, finally explained, in plain language.

[PAUSE]

Here's how it works.

First, open the app. Go to humxnmed-dot-millennials-creatives-dot-com, slash, app — or download it free from the App Store or Google Play. It works right away.

Then… just tell it what's going on. Type it, or tap the microphone and say it in your own words — a symptom, a new diagnosis, a medication, a lab result. You can even take a photo of a document. And it doesn't matter what language you speak — HumxnMed answers you in yours.

[PAUSE]

In a moment, you'll get it back in plain language. What it means, what to watch for, and good questions to bring to your doctor. Remember — this is information, not a diagnosis.

You can also keep track between visits. Log how you're feeling, add your medications, set reminders… and build a Health Passport — a private summary of your allergies, medications, and conditions that you can show any doctor, or an emergency room, with a single QR code.

[PAUSE]

And all of it stays private. Your health records live on your device — never sold, never used for ads. When you use the AI, only what you send is processed to answer you… and it isn't stored.

[PAUSE]

That's HumxnMed. Everyone you love, and everything about your health — finally in one place, and finally in plain language.

Open the app, and take a look.
