## 1. Title & tagline
**Title:** HumxnMed — Your health, finally explained. In plain language. For everyone.
**Tagline (subtitle):** A private, plain-language health companion that helps you understand your
symptoms, results, and medications — and helps clinicians at the point of care. Already live. Help us
keep it free and get it to the people who need it most.

## 2. The pitch (top of page)
Most of us have sat in a doctor's office nodding along to words we didn't understand — then gone home
and typed our symptoms into Google, only to end up more scared than before. If English isn't your first
language, or you can't afford to just "go get it checked," it's even harder.

**We built HumxnMed to fix that.** It takes what's confusing about your health — a lab result, a new
diagnosis, a medication, a bill — and explains it in plain language, in *your* language, so you can walk
into your next appointment informed instead of lost. It's **information to help you understand and
prepare — never a diagnosis, and never a replacement for your doctor.**

**It's already live and working today.** We're not asking you to fund an idea — we're asking you to help
us **keep the core free**, bring it to **more languages and more people**, and open it up to **clinicians**
so the same plain-language engine helps on both sides of the visit.

## 3. Two worlds, one mission
- **For you & your family:** understand any symptom, result, med, or bill in plain language (15+
  languages, read-aloud), track how you feel, build a private "Health Passport" for any doctor or ER,
  and get ready for your visits.
- **For clinicians:** a point-of-care companion — rapid sourced reference, dictation-to-draft notes,
  plain-language patient communication, and evidence you independently review. (Supports judgment; not
  a medical device.)

## 4. Why it's different (and why it's worth backing)
- **Private by design.** Your health records live on your device — never sold, never used for ads. When
  you use the AI, only what you send is processed to answer you, and it isn't stored by default.
- **Built for everyone.** 15+ languages, read-aloud, accessibility, and a **free core** — because
  understanding your health shouldn't depend on income or language.
- **Real sources, real codes.** Medication and terminology come from official databases (NIH RxNorm,
  LOINC, ICD-10) — not made up by an AI.
- **Already real.** Live in the App Store and on the web, used by real people.

## 5. What your backing funds

- 🩺 **The clinical path** — the compliance work (BAAs, security program) to safely bring it to clinics
  and community health workers.
- 🔧 **Reliability & scale** — infrastructure so it stays fast and free as more people join.

## 8. Risks & challenges (Kickstarter requires this — honest version)
HumxnMed is **already built and live**, which removes the biggest risk in most campaigns — we're funding
growth, not an unproven idea. Rewards are **digital** (instant delivery, no shipping). The real work
ahead is responsible expansion: bringing the clinician tools to practices requires healthcare-data
agreements and a security program, which we're pursuing methodically and will not shortcut — we'd
rather move carefully than overpromise. HumxnMed provides **information and education, not diagnosis**,
and is **not a medical device**; we keep that line firmly and design to it.

## 9. FAQ
- **Is this a diagnosis?** No. It's plain-language information to help you understand and prepare for
  your doctor. Not a medical device, not medical advice, not for emergencies (call your local emergency
  number).
- **Is my data safe?** Your records stay on your device. We don't sell data or use it for ads. When you
  use the AI, only what you send is processed to answer you, and it isn't stored by default.
- **What do I get, and when?** Digital access — delivered right after the campaign via a code you redeem
  in the app. No shipping.
- **What languages?** 15+, with read-aloud. Stretch goals add more.
- **Can clinicians use it?** Yes — a separate "clinician world." Full patient-data (HIPAA) use is being
  brought online carefully; today clinicians use it for general/de-identified support.
