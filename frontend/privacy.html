Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 18, 2026

HumxnMed AI ("we", "us", "the Service") is operated by Millennials Creatives LLC. HumxnMed AI is a health-information companion that helps people understand health topics in plain language. This policy explains what information we handle and how.

HumxnMed AI is not a medical provider. It provides general health information for educational purposes only. It is not a medical device, not a diagnosis, and not a substitute for professional medical advice. It does not handle emergencies — call your local emergency number (911 in the US) if you may be experiencing one.

1. Information we handle

  • What you type or speak: the symptoms, questions, medications, or health topics you describe so the Service can generate a briefing for you.
  • Images you choose to upload: for example a lab result or photo, used only to generate your response.
  • Account information (optional): if you create an account, your email address and a securely hashed password, handled by our authentication provider.
  • Technical data: basic, standard server logs needed to operate and secure the Service.

2. How we use it

  • To generate the health briefing or medication-interaction information you request.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the Service.
  • To respond to questions you send us.

3. AI processing — what leaves your device, and to whom

HumxnMed is local-first: your saved records live on your device. But the AI features are cloud-based, so when you use them, the specific content you submit is sent to third-party providers to generate a response. Being direct about exactly what goes where:

  • Text you submit (symptoms, questions, notes, records you ask about) → Anthropic (Claude) to generate the answer.
  • Photos you upload (e.g. a document or lab image) → Anthropic's vision model to read them.
  • Voice recordings, if you use the microphone → a speech-to-text provider (Groq, OpenAI, or ElevenLabs) to transcribe; read-aloud uses a text-to-speech provider.
  • Connected records, if you link a patient portal (Epic / MyChart via SMART-on-FHIR) → imported and parsed to answer your questions.
  • Account & plan (email + which tier you own), if you create an account → Supabase. Payments, if you buy a plan → Stripe. We store email and tier only — never your health records.
  • Optional "reviewed by a clinician": if you request a human review, the relevant content is stored so a reviewer can see it. That is the one path where you actively choose to have content saved.

We send only what is needed to answer each request, and we do not write your health content to our own database by default. These AI providers process data under their own terms; formal healthcare assurances (signed BAAs, a HIPAA program, SOC 2) are in progress — until they are complete, do not enter information you are not comfortable processing under standard commercial cloud terms, and clinicians should not enter identifiable patient information.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal or health information.
  • We do not use your health inputs for advertising.
  • We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

5. Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us operate the Service (such as hosting, AI processing, and authentication), and where required by law. These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy commitments.

6. Your choices & rights

You can use the Service without creating an account. If you have an account or have sent us information, you may request access to or deletion of that information by emailing us at the address below.

7. Data retention & security

We retain information only as long as needed to provide the Service and meet legal obligations. We take reasonable measures to protect information we receive, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
