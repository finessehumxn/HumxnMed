How to use HumxnMed

Simple steps to get going. Pick your view below.

1

Open the console

Go to the point-of-care console at humxnmed.millennialscreatives.com/console in any browser — on your phone or a computer. Nothing to install.

2

Activate your access founding clinicians

If you were given a founder code, open /redeem, type the code, and you’ll see “Clinical is activated.” No account needed — it works on any device you enter it on.

3

Pick the tool you need

Four tabs across the top: 🔎 Look up (rapid sourced reference), 📝 Document (turn rough notes into a clean draft), 💬 Explain (put the plan in plain language for the patient), and ⚖️ Weigh (considerations to weigh — you decide).

4

Type or dictate

Type in the box, or tap 🎤 Dictate and speak — take your time, pauses are fine, it won’t cut you off. Tap the mic again to stop, review the text, then tap the tool’s button (e.g. Draft note). It never sends anything on its own.

5

Read it back hands-free

Turn on 🔊 Read aloud to hear the result — useful when you’re scrubbed in or driving between rooms. 🕘 Recent re-opens results from this session (nothing is stored).

6

Sign in to keep it with you optional

Create a free account with your email + a 6-digit code so your plan follows you across devices. You never need it to use the console — it just remembers you.

1

Open the app

Go to humxnmed.millennialscreatives.com/app, or download it from the App Store / Google Play. It’s free and works right away.

2

Tell it what’s going on

Type or tap the mic and say it in your own words — a symptom, a new diagnosis, a medication, a lab result, or a photo of a document. Any language works.

3

Get it in plain language

You’ll get a clear, plain-language explanation with what to watch for and good questions for your doctor. It’s information, not a diagnosis.

4

Keep track between visits

Log symptoms and meds, set reminders, and build a Health Passport — a private summary (allergies, meds, conditions) you can show any doctor or ER by QR code.

5

It stays private

Your health records live on your device — never sold, never used for ads. When you use the AI, only what you send is processed to answer you, and it isn’t stored.
